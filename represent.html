I'm a New Zealand-born, London-based film and TV director and writer seeking representation. I've written and directed two feature films, a TV series, and three novels under a pen name. I am highly disciplined and collaborative.

My wheelhouse is "energetic comedies with heart", character-driven stories about status, belonging and social power, wrapped up (usually) in a comedic mystery plot.

Notable Work

CAMP BE BETTER (2024)

6 episode teen dramedy. Funded by NZ On Air and released on TVNZ+.

Roles: CREATED • CO-WRITER • CO-DIRECTOR • EDITOR • "THOMAS"

Praised by The Spinoff as "a quirky teen drama" thanks to its energetic script, sharp one-liners, and keen satire of privilege. Style draws comparisons to Sex Education, Skins, and The O.C.

DEAD (2020)

Horror comedy feature film.

Roles: CO-WRITER • DIRECTOR • PRODUCER • "TAGG"

Rotten Tomatoes: 86%. Critics praise it as "tremendously enjoyable" with "a lot of personality and heart." FrightFest, The Hollywood News, and Nerdly acclaim it as "a shining example of perfect horror comedy" with a "tender heart" and "inclusive queer component."

CHRONESTHESIA (2016) - Love and Time Travel in the US

Indie time travel feature film.

Roles: WRITER • DIRECTOR • EDITOR • "DAN"

Critics praised it as "a total head-trip in all the right ways," drawing comparisons to Charlie Kaufman for its uniquely layered approach. Lauded for genre-defying tone and emotional complexity.

Notable for its "sun-drenched cinematography," smart editing, creative vérité storytelling, and superb ensemble work (featuring Julian Dennison and Michelle Ny). Makes Wellington feel like a character in its own right.

Official selection at NZ International Film Festival, Shanghai International, Austin Film Festival.

Young Adult Mystery Novels (as Emerson Black)

While making films and working as an editor, I've been pursuing another passion: writing novels under the pen name Emerson Black. I love books and writing, so during Covid I decided to self-publish. I'd written three full-length novels before and hadn't published them, but after writing the Seen in Silverbridge series, I felt like it was time to get a professional editor, cover designer, and put some books out into the world. I continue to write books, spending two hours every morning before my workday starts on the next one.
